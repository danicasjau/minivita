Life trapped: PRESENTATION OF A VIRTUAL CHARACTER

Daniel Casadevall

Mini Vita is an intelligent character with the ability to interact with the user. It has its own memory, fluid movement based on conversation, and interacts with the environment. It can run on a modern home computer.

DESCRIPTION

After turning 35, Tom wakes up in a white box—a locked room where there is only one voice: yours.

Tom remembers his entire life and wants to escape, to return to his family, but he cannot. His existence, his memories, and emotions only exist inside the computer.

Outside the box, Tom is nobody; he does not exist, he cannot exist. But he believes he does, and he will do everything possible to get out.

Poor Tom.

VISION

Mini Vita takes painting and animation into a new dimension: it allows artists to create not only the physical aspects of characters but also to define their minds, memories, and emotions.

Characters can remember their mothers, the house where they were born, and their first loves—moments that never actually existed.

The goal is not just to observe, but to feel the despair of the small trapped man and to explore new frontiers between technology and art.

Mini Vita proposes a new way of understanding digital narrative: more intimate, emotional, and interactive.

VERSIONS
Version 01


Real-time local operation, unstable movement, memory not implemented, imprecise voice.

Version 02


Memory implemented, more precise voice, stable movement. No 3D environment interaction.
